description=[h2]✨ What is this[/h2]
description=Overlays "map images rendered from the actual in-game view" onto the B42 world map and corner mini-map, replacing the vanilla vector-line style. Built on the engine's native ImagePyramid: multi-level LOD for smooth zooming, low VRAM, and what-you-see-is-what-you-get.
description=
description=[h2]🧰 Main features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Image-based world map & mini-map[/b] — matches the in-game view; switch back to vanilla vector style anytime
description=[*] [b]Navigation target + road-following route[/b]: right-click to set a target — a route is computed along actual roads (auto-replans when you stray; deep-wilderness targets route to the nearest road), with flag + edge arrow + distance, auto-clears on arrival; share to your faction and teammates get the route too
description=[*] [b]Map search[/b]: magnifier button or right-click "Search map…" — coordinates, street names ([u]English and translated names both work[/u]) or facility categories (pharmacy, gun store etc. across the 20 categories), sorted by distance; jump to the world map (gold pulsing marker) or set as navigation target directly
description=[*] [b]Mini-map hotkey + floating icon[/b]: default / (rebindable), works even when the sandbox disables the mini-map; the always-on icon toggles the map (left-click) and ghost mode (right-click), drag to reposition
description=[*] [b]Free sizing[/b]: four presets + edge-drag resizing with memory; the button bar stays put — no hover-expanding
description=[*] [b]Live zombie dots[/b] (off by default): real-time zombie positions with adjustable color / size / opacity / cap; vanilla heatmap toggle included
description=[*] [b]Animal & vehicle icons[/b] (off by default): separate wild/livestock toggles, species and vehicle-category filters (the compatibility pack adds dogs, horses and more); two icon styles, colorblind-friendly palette
description=[*] [b]World-map icons[/b]: the same zombie/animal/vehicle icons on the world map (M), four independent toggles
description=[*] [b]Street names + safehouse outlines[/b]: street names on the corner mini-map (vanilla never had them); your safehouse green, others red
description=[*] [b]Player coordinates + one-click copy[/b]: x, y, z at the bottom of both maps; XY button copies, right-click copies any pointed spot — paste straight into /teleportto
description=[*] [b]Ghost mode[/b]: clicks and wheel pass through to the game while the map turns semi-transparent — enlarge it into a permanent overlay that never blocks play; hotkey '
description=[*] [b]Free look[/b]: drag to inspect and stay there, click once to snap back to the player
description=[*] [b]Unified settings window[/b] (gear button): collapsible two-column sections, every change applies and saves instantly — no ESC menu needed
description=[*] [b]Server sandbox controls[/b]: disable individual icons, cap display distances, four livestock-visibility levels, faction-sharing toggle — changes apply live
description=[*] [b]Singleplayer & multiplayer[/b]: only visualizes data the client already receives — no extra intel; Traditional/Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]⚠️ -debug users[/b]: HOME is a hidden engine render-debug key (halves FPS). This mod defaults to / and auto-migrates old bindings; a conflict shows an orange warning bar.
description=
description=[h2]🧩 Map mod support[/h2]
description=Pair with the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763914102]MOD Maps pack[/url]: mini-map images + boundary outlines for many map mods, auto-detected — shown only when the matching map mod is enabled.
description=Map mod authors can ship their own support: render a [b]minidoracat_minimap.pyramid.zip[/b] into [b]media/minimap/[/b] — [u]no Lua needed[/u].
description=
description=[h2]🗺️ Built-in POIs + zone layer API[/h2]
description=1669 points of interest across 20 categories on the vanilla map (military/medical/commercial/industrial etc., each color-coded): tinted-silhouette icons (full-color optional) or translucent blocks, per-category toggles in the settings window. Positions come straight from official map files; categories are derived from official room [u]loot types[/u] — they may disagree with the map's zoning colors (residential/commercial legend), the actual loot is what counts.
description=Basement facilities (B42 basements — a bar under a house, underground armories) get a "↓" corner mark on the icon and a "(basement)" suffix in search results — the building above ground may be something else.
description=A `registerZoneProvider` zone-rendering framework is also included; with the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3768276209]Zones addon[/url] it displays server-defined zones (zones.json).
description=
